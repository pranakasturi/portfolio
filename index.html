Core Expertise

1. Data Cleaning & Validation

Prepare business data for analysis and reporting through structured cleaning and validation workflows.

  • Identify duplicate records, missing values, inconsistent formats, and data quality issues.
  • Automate data cleaning, validation rules, and before-and-after quality checks.
  • Generate data quality summaries and reports to support review and correction.

2. MIS & Reporting Automation

Simplify recurring reporting processes by connecting data preparation, KPI calculations, and report generation.

  • Automate weekly and monthly reporting workflows.
  • Consolidate data from multiple files and configured sources.
  • Generate management reports and KPI summaries for business review.

3. Custom Desktop Applications

Develop Windows desktop applications around specific business requirements and internal workflows.

  • Build tools for data processing, reporting, validation, and business operations.
  • Use local databases and file-based workflows where appropriate.
  • Design offline-capable applications for workflows that require local data processing, with security measures suited to the deployment environment.

4. Business Dashboards & Data Analytics

Turn business data into useful summaries that support monitoring and decision-making.

  • Develop dashboards for sales, receivables, operational performance, and other business KPIs.
  • Analyze trends, compare reporting periods, and highlight data requiring attention.
  • Use Python-based visualizations and Power BI where appropriate to the project.

About Me

Fourteen years of simplifying complexity—from the classroom to corporate software.

Complexity is only a barrier if you don’t know how to break it down.

My journey began in Computer Science education, where I spent over 14 years teaching programming and helping students understand technical concepts through structured, practical explanations.

Over time, my interest in programming and problem-solving extended beyond teaching into developing software applications and working with data. This transition allowed me to apply my technical knowledge to real-world workflows and business requirements.

My approach to development is shaped by both experiences: understanding a problem carefully, breaking it into manageable parts, and choosing a solution that is practical and maintainable.

I continue to explore data analysis, machine learning, and AI, with an interest in how these technologies can be applied thoughtfully to everyday business problems.

Outside of development, I enjoy exploring ways to make numbers, logic, and problem-solving easier to understand.
